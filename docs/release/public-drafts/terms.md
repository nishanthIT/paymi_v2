# Paymi terms — unpublished working draft

Operator: Kaliam. Contact: support@paymi.co.uk. Intended users: shop owners and employees 13+. UK public launch; India testing. This release is free; no subscription is offered. No invented registered address, governing-law clause, licence ownership, liability waiver or certification is included.

Private workspace access is granted by the authorized shop owner. Downloading Paymi does not authorize access to another shop. Use your own account and safeguard credentials. Enter and share only information you are authorized to use. Avoid unnecessary personal details, particularly about customers and children. Owners remain responsible for appropriate staff permissions and the accuracy of their operational records.

Community rules implemented in /chat-safety prohibit harassment, threats, sexual/child-exploitative/hateful/illegal material, scams, spam and unauthorized disclosure. Global chat spans registered users; keep private business information in authorized team conversations. Report content or senders using the message flag; blocking restricts communication, not business permissions. Kaliam moderators can remove reported content. Contact support for safety concerns or moderation challenges.

Personal account deletion is distinct from shared workspace deletion. Existing authorized co-owners remain; staff are not automatically promoted. Contact support about recovery, ownership or privacy. Do not send passwords by email.

Before publication: operator/legal review of age-appropriate acceptance, contractual capacity/employment roles, data responsibilities, acceptable content, intellectual property rights, service limitations, suspension/appeal, closure, jurisdiction and change notification. Approve operating commitments rather than promising unstaffed response times. This document cannot establish those facts itself.
