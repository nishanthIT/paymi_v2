# Paymi privacy policy — unpublished draft

Paymi is operated by Kaliam for shop owners and employees. For support, privacy questions and requests, contact support@paymi.co.uk. The intended audience is 13 and above. Private shop workspaces require an account and appropriate permissions.

Paymi handles account names, email addresses, passwords in hashed form and staff/workspace relationships. It also handles information you or authorized colleagues enter: lists and stock, tasks, operational logs, messages, photos, documents, price reports and business records. Some records can contain information about staff, customers or other people. Avoid entering unnecessary personal information. Label drafts, cached records and exported files may be stored on your device.

We use this information to provide the requested workspace and communication functions, verify and recover accounts, maintain security, provide support and handle reports. Team information is available to authorized workspace members. ALL Chat is visible beyond your shop to registered users. Service administrators can handle support and moderation. System printing/sharing sends selected material to the destination you choose.

The service uses backend hosting, a PostgreSQL database and transactional email. **Before publication: identify all actual hosting, database, email, content/image and logging providers; verify their locations, roles, transfers and protections.** External websites and image/content providers may receive network information when accessed; complete the destination audit before specifying tracking/sharing practices.

Camera scanning and choosing photos are optional actions. Manual barcode entry is available. Account recovery, support information and personal deletion requests can be reached without access to a private workspace. There is no subscription checkout in this release.

Your account and personal content are handled separately from shared shop records and other people's accounts. Personal references in retained shared records require review. Workspace deletion is a separate authorized action. A request receipt lets you check progress; submission is not confirmation of completed erasure.
